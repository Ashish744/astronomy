Our story

Astronomy, without the barrier

Stackly started with one telescope shared between three friends. It's now a network built to put a real, working observatory in reach of anyone with a clear question about the sky.

Scroll down to explore the network, the mission, and the clear skies ahead.
The problem

Good skies are rarely where people are

Light pollution, weather, and the cost of equipment keep most people from ever seeing deep space clearly — even devoted hobbyists.

The idea

Share the hardware, not just the photos

Instead of another gallery app, we wired real telescopes to the internet so anyone could point them, not just look at someone else's result.

Today

A global, member-run network

42 telescopes, 63 countries, and a community that keeps teaching each other how to get more out of every clear night.
