Privacy Policy

Last updated August 4, 2026

1. What we collect

We collect the account details you provide (name, email), session data from telescope bookings, and basic usage analytics to keep the network running smoothly.

2. How we use it

Your data is used to operate your account, schedule sessions, personalize your archive, and communicate service updates. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

3. Observation data

Captured images and session logs are stored in your archive and remain yours. Sharing to the community feed is always opt-in.

4. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in and remember your preferences. No third-party advertising trackers are used on Stackly.

5. Your choices

You can export or delete your archive at any time from account settings, or contact support to request full account deletion.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@stackly.example or through the contact page.
