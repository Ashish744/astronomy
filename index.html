How it works

From your desk to deep space in four steps

01 / Reserve

Book a telescope slot

Pick an observatory from the network by location, aperture, or the object you want to see, then reserve a live time slot in minutes.

02 / Frame

Point and frame your target

Search the catalog or drop a coordinate — Stackly slews the mount and frames the shot while you watch the feed load in real time.

03 / Capture

Capture in real time

Stack exposures live, adjust filters, and pull in calibration frames automatically as the session runs.

04 / Share

Save, process and share

Export a processed image straight to your archive, or publish it to the community feed for others to explore.

Milestones

Built in the open, one launch at a time

2021

Stackly begins as a weekend project

Three amateur astronomers wire a single 8" reflector to the internet to share it with friends.

2022

First public observatory partnership

A dark-sky reserve in Chile joins the network, bringing southern-hemisphere targets online.

2023

Community missions launch

Citizen-science campaigns for variable stars and near-earth objects open to every member.

2025

42 telescopes, 63 countries

The network crosses six continents, logging its 100,000th shared observation.

2026

Learning paths and mobile app

Guided lessons and a redesigned dashboard make Stackly a full start-to-finish learning platform.
